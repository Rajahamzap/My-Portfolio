FAQ

#Frequently Asked Questions

What SEO services do you offer?

I offer technical SEO, on-page SEO, off-page SEO and link building, local SEO, semantic SEO and entity optimization, GEO and AI search optimization, SEO audits and competitor analysis, and keyword research and content strategy.

Which industries do you work with?

SaaS and technology, artificial intelligence, VPN and cybersecurity, healthcare and medical services, medical lead generation, affiliate and content websites, and service-based businesses.

Do you provide technical SEO audits?

Yes. A technical audit reviews crawlability, indexation, site structure, performance, Core Web Vitals and structured data. Broader SEO audits also cover content, on-page and off-page opportunities and include competitor research.

Do you offer local SEO services?

Yes. Local SEO covers Google Business Profile optimization, location landing pages, local keyword research, citations and local authority building. See local SEO for details.

What is GEO and AI search optimization?

GEO is about improving a brand's visibility in AI-powered search platforms. It uses answer-focused content, entity optimization, topical authority, authoritative mentions and AI search visibility analysis. It does not guarantee rankings or citations. See GEO and AI search optimization.

How do you measure SEO performance?

I use Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4 and tools such as Ahrefs and Semrush, and look at the measures that matter to the business, such as organic traffic, visibility, enquiries and conversions. Rankings alone do not show business success.

How long does SEO take to produce results?

It depends on the competition, the website's current condition, the content and its authority. SEO is generally gradual, and I do not promise specific results or timeframes. After an audit I can give a more realistic picture for your situation.

Do you work with international businesses?

Yes. I work remotely from Karachi, Pakistan, so location is not a barrier to working together. Tell me about your markets and goals on the contact page.

Tell me about your website, your audience and what you want to achieve, and we can discuss how SEO could support it.
