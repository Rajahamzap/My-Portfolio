Local SEO Services

Local SEO helps a business appear when nearby customers search for what it offers. It combines the website, the Google Business Profile and the wider web so that each describes the same real business accurately.

Discuss Your ProjectExplore My Work

Scope

#What this service covers

  • Google Business Profile optimization for eligible businesses.
  • Location-specific landing pages that provide genuinely useful, distinct information.
  • Local keyword research and search intent.
  • Local citations with consistent name, address and phone details.
  • Local internal linking and schema markup.
  • Local performance measurement in Google Business Profile, Search Console and Google Analytics 4.

Approach

#How I approach it

  1. Check which locations and services the business genuinely covers.
  2. Build location pages only where there is real, useful content to publish.
  3. Align the profile, citations and website details.
  4. Measure calls, clicks and enquiries, not rankings alone.
