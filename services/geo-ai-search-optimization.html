GEO & AI Search Optimization Services

AI-powered search experiences answer questions directly and draw on a range of sources. This service helps a brand become a clear, credible and well-understood source for the questions that matter to its audience.

Discuss Your ProjectExplore My Work

Scope

#What this service covers

  • Answer-focused content that is easy to extract and understand.
  • Entity optimization, so the brand and its topics are clearly defined.
  • Topical authority built across a subject area.
  • Authoritative third-party mentions and reputation.
  • AI search visibility analysis: reviewing how the brand appears in AI-generated answers.
  • Technical foundations that keep content accessible.

Approach

#How I approach it

  1. Review how the brand and its competitors currently appear in AI-powered search.
  2. Improve content, entities and structure where the gaps are.
  3. Strengthen third-party validation through mentions and reviews.
  4. Re-check visibility over time, as AI search keeps changing.
