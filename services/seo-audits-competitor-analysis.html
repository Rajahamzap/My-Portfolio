SEO Audit & Competitor Analysis Services

An SEO audit shows where a website currently stands and what is holding it back. Competitor research shows where the opportunities are, so the recommendations are prioritized rather than a long, undifferentiated list.

Discuss Your ProjectExplore My Work

Scope

#What this service covers

  • Technical audit: crawlability, indexation, site structure, performance and structured data.
  • Content audit: quality, gaps, overlap and search intent alignment.
  • On-page audit: metadata, headings, internal links and page structure.
  • Off-page audit: backlink profile and brand mentions.
  • Competitor research across keywords, content and backlinks.
  • A prioritized set of findings and recommendations.

Approach

#How I approach it

  1. Crawl the website and gather Search Console and analytics data.
  2. Review the pages and the competitors in the same search results.
  3. Group the findings by impact and effort.
  4. Turn them into a prioritized roadmap.
