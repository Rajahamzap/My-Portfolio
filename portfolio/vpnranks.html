The Challenge

VPNRanks competes in one of the most contested affiliate niches online. The goal was to grow organic visibility, strengthen topical relevance and support affiliate revenue through SEO processes that could scale across a large content library.

  • A highly competitive VPN, privacy and cybersecurity search landscape where rankings are hard to win and easy to lose.
  • The same topic can be searched with informational, commercial or transactional intent, and each needs a different kind of page.
  • A large content library that needed consistent on-page standards rather than one-off fixes.
  • Strong country-specific demand, especially for streaming and sports queries, where a generic page does not match what searchers want.
  • Seasonal demand spikes around major sporting events that affiliate content had to be ready for.
  • Authority gaps against established competitors that had to be closed through quality backlinks.

SEO Strategy & Implementation

The work, organized by discipline. Open any block to see what I did, the approach, how it was carried out and why it mattered.

Keyword research & search intent mappingClassifying keywords by search intent and deciding which type of page should answer each one.

What I worked on

Classifying keywords by search intent and deciding which type of page should answer each one.

The strategy

Keywords were grouped as informational, commercial or transactional, then matched to the format that serves that intent best: blogs, comparison pages or commercial landing pages.

How it was implemented

Every keyword group was assigned a content format and a primary and secondary intent before pages were written or updated.

Why it mattered

On an affiliate site a page that answers the wrong intent attracts the wrong visitors. Intent mapping kept research-stage and buying-stage searches on the pages built for them.

On-page SEO & content structureA standardized keyword placement framework covering meta titles, meta descriptions, H1s, introductions, releva…

What I worked on

A standardized keyword placement framework covering meta titles, meta descriptions, H1s, introductions, relevant headings and contextual mentions.

The strategy

Content was structured around primary and secondary search intent. The primary intent was answered immediately after the introduction, followed by supporting sections under a logical H2 and H3 hierarchy.

How it was implemented

The framework was applied as a repeatable standard so large numbers of pages could be optimized consistently.

Why it mattered

A repeatable standard made optimization scalable across a large content library and kept quality consistent from page to page.

  • Meta titles and descriptions
  • H1s and introductions
  • Logical H2 and H3 hierarchies
  • Primary intent answered straight after the introduction
Semantic SEO & content gap analysisExpanding content with semantically related keywords, entities, supporting concepts and relevant subtopics, gu…

What I worked on

Expanding content with semantically related keywords, entities, supporting concepts and relevant subtopics, guided by competitor content gap analysis.

The strategy

Instead of repeating one phrase, pages were broadened to cover the topic the way searchers and competing pages do, filling the gaps that competitors covered and existing pages did not.

How it was implemented

Competitor content was reviewed for missing subtopics and entities, and the gaps were added to existing pages or planned into new content.

Why it mattered

Greater topical depth and content quality across the library strengthened relevance for competitive VPN and privacy queries.

Region-specific content strategyCreating country-specific content instead of relying on one generic page per topic.

What I worked on

Creating country-specific content instead of relying on one generic page per topic.

The strategy

A generic keyword such as "How to Watch Rugby World Cup" ignores the fact that viewing options differ by country. The strategy was to target the regional versions of the query so each page matched local search intent and streaming availability.

How it was implemented

Each regional page was written for the situation in its own country, reflecting how people there search and what they can access.

Why it mattered

Country-specific queries carry clear intent. A page built for that exact situation is a more precise answer than one broad article.

  • How to Watch Rugby World Cup in the USA
  • How to Watch Rugby World Cup in the UK
  • How to Watch Rugby World Cup in France
  • How to Watch Rugby World Cup in Germany
Seasonal & event-based SEOPlanning and optimizing content around major sporting events.

What I worked on

Planning and optimizing content around major sporting events.

The strategy

Large events create predictable spikes in search demand. Planning content around them let the site target that demand when it appeared, and gave affiliate content a clear commercial opportunity.

How it was implemented

Event content was planned and optimized for the search demand around each tournament.

Why it mattered

Seasonal demand created affiliate opportunities that evergreen pages alone would not have captured.

  • Rugby World Cup
  • Cricket World Cup
  • Tennis Grand Slams
Technical SEOKeeping a large, content-heavy site crawlable, indexable and free from duplication problems.

What I worked on

Keeping a large, content-heavy site crawlable, indexable and free from duplication problems.

The strategy

Technical health was treated as a foundation: search engines needed to find, understand and index the right pages without confusion from duplicates or broken paths.

How it was implemented

Regular technical audits and monitoring covered the areas listed below.

Why it mattered

On a site with a large content library, small technical issues multiply. Ongoing monitoring protected the work done on content and links.

  • XML sitemap management
  • Robots.txt optimization
  • Crawlability and indexation monitoring
  • Canonical tags
  • Redirect management
  • Duplicate content identification
  • Structured data
  • Technical SEO audits
  • Core Web Vitals and mobile usability monitoring
Off-page SEO & authority buildingBacklink analysis and outreach to close authority gaps against competitors.

What I worked on

Backlink analysis and outreach to close authority gaps against competitors.

The strategy

Competitor backlink and link gap analysis showed where authority was missing. Outreach then focused on qualified publishers, content-led link acquisition and a natural anchor text profile.

How it was implemented

Link prospects were qualified before outreach, publisher relationships were managed over time, and backlink quality was evaluated rather than judged on volume.

Why it mattered

Authority is a major ranking factor in the VPN niche. Quality-led link building supported the content and technical work.

  • Competitor backlink analysis
  • Link gap analysis
  • Guest post outreach
  • Link prospect qualification
  • Publisher relationship management
  • Anchor text diversification
  • Backlink quality evaluation
  • Content-led link acquisition
  • Authority-focused link-building campaigns
  • Digital PR
