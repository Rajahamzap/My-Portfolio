The Challenge

Medical billing and revenue cycle management providers compete for the same commercial searches, so a newer site needs both a strong foundation and a clear reason for each page to exist.

  • Building a sustainable SEO foundation for a newer lead-generation website.
  • Competing for commercial searches in the medical billing and RCM space.
  • Keeping service pages focused on conversion while still building authority through supporting content.
  • Creating an architecture that can grow without becoming disorganized.
  • Developing authority within a constrained budget.
  • Preparing content for AI-driven discovery as well as traditional search.

Site architecture & keyword strategyOrganizing the site into three content types, each serving a different stage of search intent.

What I worked on

Organizing the site into three content types, each serving a different stage of search intent.

The strategy

Business pages target buyers, depth blogs build topical authority, and best-of and comparison blogs capture people who are evaluating providers.

How it was implemented

Keywords were assigned to one of the three categories, and the categories were connected through internal links.

Why it mattered

Different stages of the buyer journey need different pages. A clear structure lets the site serve all of them and scale in an orderly way.

  • Business Pages
  • Depth Blogs
  • Best-of and Comparison Blogs: for example Best Medical Billing Companies and Best RCM Companies
  • Relevant healthcare billing commercial topics

Standardizing how pages are researched, written and optimized.

Why it mattered

Standardized processes keep optimization consistent as a site grows.
