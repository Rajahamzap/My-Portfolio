The Challenge

AI and technology is a fast-moving subject area where new tools, brands and terms appear constantly. The goal was to grow organic visibility and be recognized as a credible source across the topic.

  • A crowded field of AI and technology publishers competing for the same queries.
  • Constantly changing subject matter, so existing content needed regular refreshes to stay relevant.
  • Search that is increasingly about tools, brands and products (entities), not just keywords.
  • The need to show topical depth and credibility across a broad subject area.
  • Building authority through editorial links and research-led content, not volume.
  • A growing need to structure content so AI search platforms can understand and extract it.

On-page SEO & content strategyMapping keywords to search intent, then structuring and refreshing content around it.

What I worked on

Mapping keywords to search intent, then structuring and refreshing content around it.

The strategy

Each page was built around one primary intent with supporting secondary targets, a clear structure and content that covered what competing pages covered and more.

How it was implemented

Content gap analysis guided what to add. Semantic keywords were integrated naturally, existing content was refreshed, and internal links connected related pages.

Why it mattered

Aligning structure and depth with intent, and keeping content current, is what keeps pages competitive in a fast-moving topic.

  • Keyword intent mapping
  • Primary and secondary keyword targeting
  • Content structure optimization
  • Competitor content gap analysis
  • Semantic keyword integration
  • Content refreshes
  • Strategic internal linking
  • Search intent alignment
Entity & semantic SEO, topical authorityOptimizing content around the AI tools, brands, products, technologies and related entities that searchers act…

What I worked on

Optimizing content around the AI tools, brands, products, technologies and related entities that searchers actually look for.

The strategy

Instead of relying only on exact-match keywords, content was written to cover the entities and concepts that define each topic, which builds a more complete picture of subject coverage.

How it was implemented

Entities and related concepts were identified for each topic and worked into content and internal linking to develop depth across the subject area.

Why it mattered

Search engines increasingly interpret content through entities. Clear entity coverage and topical depth support authority for AI and technology topics.
