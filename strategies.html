How the disciplines fit together

#Overall SEO Strategy Framework

Technical SEO, research, content architecture, on-page optimization, internal linking and off-page authority work together rather than operating independently.

An interconnected framework, not a rigid sequence

A technical issue can undermine good content, and strong content can go unnoticed without internal links and authority signals. I treat the disciplines as one connected system and move between them as the data and the business goals require.

How each discipline contributes to the framework
DisciplineRole in the frameworkHow it connects to the rest
Technical SEO foundationMakes the website crawlable, indexable and easy to useEverything else depends on search engines and AI systems being able to reach and process the content
Keyword and intent researchIdentifies what people search for and what they expect to findInforms the content architecture and the on-page structure of every page
Content architectureDecides which pages exist and how topics are grouped into pillar and supporting contentShapes the internal linking and gives on-page work a structure to follow
On-page and semantic optimizationStructures each page around intent, entities and related topicsGives pages the depth that authority signals can then reinforce
Internal linkingConnects related pages and passes authority to important onesTurns individual pages into a connected topical resource
Off-page authorityBuilds credibility through backlinks, editorial coverage, reviews and discussionsReinforces the content and brand signals created by the other disciplines
Organic and AI search visibilityThe outcome: rankings, mentions and citationsResults feed back into research and optimization, so the framework is a continuous loop
